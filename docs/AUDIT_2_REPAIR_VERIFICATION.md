## Acceptance still open

A physical phone pass must cover the owner’s media, real Back gestures and keyboard/picker ownership, prolonged memory and thermal pressure, source grant loss, and Android reclaiming the process while a separate picker remains foreground. Distinct URI aliases and provider-specific partial writes need controlled provider verification. Commercial release, broader professional tool parity and the separate Video workspace have their own [release gates](RELEASE_GATE.md).
